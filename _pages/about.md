I am a PhD student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Oklahoma. My research focuses on network optimization, supply chain resilience, and data-driven methods for defense and logistics systems.

Prior to this, I completed my Master's degree at Texas State University, where my research centered on mathematical optimization and space habitation logistics.

### Research Interests
* **Network & Supply Chain Optimization**
* **Predict-then-Optimize & Data-Driven Decision Making**
* **Mixed-Integer Nonlinear & Stochastic Programming**
